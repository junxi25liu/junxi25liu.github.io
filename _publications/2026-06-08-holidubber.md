[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09098)

HoliDubber approaches video dubbing holistically: rather than dubbing speech alone, it uses text-guided audio synthesis to reconstruct complex acoustic scenes, so that speech, ambience, and sound events are handled together.
