Xiquan Li, **Junxi Liu**, Wenxi Chen, Haina Zhu, Ziyang Ma, Xie Chen

[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11661) / [code](https://github.com/xiquan-li/Resonate) / [demo](https://resonatedemo.github.io/)

Resonate applies online reinforcement learning to a flow-matching text-to-audio model, using large audio language models to provide reward signals instead of relying on human annotation. This closes the loop between generation and evaluation, improving the semantic alignment between the text prompt and the generated audio.
