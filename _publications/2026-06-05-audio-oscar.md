Yifan Duan, Qixiang Xu, Hengtao Wu, Zhanxun Liu, Wenhao Guan, **Junxi Liu**, Ziyang Ma, Kelu Xu, Xie Chen

[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07397)

Audio-Oscar treats complex audio scene creation as a multi-agent problem, splitting the task into generation, orchestration, and refinement so that long, multi-event soundscapes can be assembled and iteratively improved rather than synthesized in a single pass.
