<p class="lede">I work on audio generation — making it faster, more controllable, and closer to what people actually intend to hear.</p>

<p class="meta">
  <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=r9BSOjgAAAAJ&amp;hl=en">Scholar</a>
  <a href="https://github.com/junxi25liu">GitHub</a>
</p>

Undergraduate in Software Engineering at [UESTC](https://en.uestc.edu.cn/). Research intern at [X-LANCE Lab](https://x-lance.github.io), [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](http://en.sjtu.edu.cn), advised by [Prof. Xie Chen](https://chenxie95.github.io).

Most of my work sits at three points: cutting the sampling cost of flow- and diffusion-based audio models so a good sample takes one step instead of fifty; giving people intent-level control over what gets generated and edited; and using preference data and reinforcement learning to align generators with human judgement — plus the benchmarks needed to tell whether any of it worked.
